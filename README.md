**[iEditPDF](https://www.ieditpdf.in)** is a comprehensive, privacy-first online web suite designed to manipulate, convert, and manage PDF documents and images directly within your browser.

No installation required, no subscriptions, and your sensitive files are processed securely on your device without being uploaded to remote servers.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Tools

You can access all tools for free at [ieditpdf.in](https://www.ieditpdf.in):

### 📑 PDF Management
- **[Merge PDF](https://www.ieditpdf.in/tools/merge-pdf)** – Combine multiple PDF files into one ordered document.
- **[Split PDF](https://www.ieditpdf.in/tools/split-pdf)** – Extract specific pages or divide a document.
- **[Compress PDF](https://www.ieditpdf.in/tools/compress-pdf)** – Reduce PDF file size while preserving document quality.
- **[PDF Editor](https://www.ieditpdf.in/tools/edit-pdf)** – Add text, annotations, watermarks, and signatures.
- **[Protect & Unlock PDF](https://www.ieditpdf.in)** – Add or remove passwords securely.

### 🔄 Conversions
- **PDF to Word / Images** – High-accuracy document format conversion.
- **Images to PDF** – Convert JPG, PNG, and WebP into organized PDF books.

### 🖼️ Image Utilities
- **Background Remover** – Remove photo backgrounds automatically.
- **Image Merger & Compressor** – Optimize and stitch visual assets.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

Your documents remain **100% private**:
- Files are processed locally on your client machine using modern WebAssembly and JavaScript engines.
- No files are stored or logged on third-party servers.
